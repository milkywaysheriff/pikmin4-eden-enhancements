# Pikmin 4 — Eden Enhancements 1.0 beta 1

Optional mods for **Pikmin 4 update 1.1.1 on Eden**.

## Included

- Higher frame rate targets: **60, 120 or 180 FPS**.
- Pikmin movement fixes for following and jumping onto Oatchi at higher FPS.
- Faster camera rotation: **+12.5%, +25%, +50%, +75% or +100%**.
- Ultrawide support: **3440×1440 or 5120×2160**.
- Optional **Chromatic Aberration OFF** and **Dynamic Resolution Lock**.

## Download and install

Download **`Pikmin4_1.1.1_Eden_Enhancements_1.0-beta1.zip`** for the mods. The **`_Sources.zip`** attachment is for development. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the attachment checksums.

Close Eden, back up equivalent old mods and copy only your selected `P4 - ...` folders from inside the numbered groups into the game's mod location. Enable them in the game's properties.

Choose at most one FPS, one camera speed and one ultrawide resolution. Each FPS option needs both main and SDK files and the separate **Pikmin Movement and Timers** mod. For ultrawide use Eden **1×**, **GPU High**, Stretch to Window and a matching image area. Keep emulation speed **100%**.

## Beta limits

The local play setup uses 60 FPS and 5120×2160. Movement improvements have positive feedback. 120 FPS is experimental; 180 FPS and the current 3440×1440 variant have not been play-tested locally. Test coverage for all options is documented in the download.

FPS targets depend on performance; viewport sizes do not establish the actual internal 3D rendering resolution. Other game updates, emulators and platforms are unverified. Full installation, removal and credits are included. No game executables or personal profiles are distributed.
